    Facilitated Pre-Disaster Risk Assessment (PDRA) & Scenario-Building Meeting (Nov 08). Issued Memo Advisories regarding precautionary measures and pre-emptive evacuations. Conducted maintenance check and inventory of search and rescue equipment.

    Activated Emergency Operations Center (EOC) on a 24/7 basis. Conducted local PDRA to identify high-risk barangays. Instructed barangays to prepare for possible pre-emptive evacuation. Conducted pruning operations on overgrown trees.
